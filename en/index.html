Understand your business

Before touching anything, we understand how the work really gets done.

  • We talk to the people who do the work, not only to those who manage it.
  • We ask each person which part of their job they would rather never do again.
  • We measure with you how long each process takes today, instead of estimating it.
  • If two systems mean different things by “customer”, that gets resolved before anything else.

The ontology is the map of your company: what exists, how it all relates and who decides what.

Automate step by step

On day one we ask for data and access, not another meeting.

  1. DataData first.
  2. WorkflowsThen the workflows.
  3. AIAI comes last.
  • We start with one specific workflow, get it running and move on to the next.
  • We prioritise by hours saved, not by how impressive the demo looks.
  • If a process is broken, we fix it before automating it.
  • We show you progress every week, not a report at the end.

Know Foundry inside out

We work in Foundry every day. We know where it gets complicated.

  • We design the ontology with what comes next in mind, not just the first use case.
  • Before migrating anything important, we check that Foundry responds on time without driving up compute costs.
  • Your team works with us from the start instead of receiving a black box at the end.
  • If Foundry is not the right tool for a problem, we tell you.

Own the outcome

Every project has an agreed start and an agreed end.

  • Everything we learn about your operation is written down.
  • Once it works, we stay close to maintain and improve it, for as long as it is useful to you.
  • You decide how much you do with your own team and how much with us.

We don’t finish with a recommendation. We finish when it works.

Our philosophy

Four ideas behind every project.

Understand your business

Before touching anything, we understand how the work really gets done.

  • We talk to the people who do the work, not only to those who manage it.
  • We ask each person which part of their job they would rather never do again.
  • If two systems mean different things by “customer”, that gets resolved before anything else.

Automate step by step

On day one we ask for data and access, not another meeting.

  • We prioritise by hours saved, not by how impressive the demo looks.
  • What is a rule gets automated. What needs judgement is still decided by a person.
  • If a process is broken, we fix it before automating it.

Know Foundry inside out

We work in Foundry every day. We know where it gets complicated.

  • Before migrating anything important, we check that it responds on time without driving up compute costs.
  • Every piece of data is written in one place only, so the silos don’t come back.
  • If Foundry is not the right tool for a problem, we tell you.

Own the outcome

Every project has an agreed start and an agreed end.

  • Everything we learn about your operation is written down.
  • After launch we measure whether people use it, not just whether it works.
  • You decide how much you do with your own team and how much with us.

When we build something, we don’t finish with a recommendation. We finish when it works.

Questions

What people usually ask us.

  • Do I need Palantir Foundry?

    We mostly work in Foundry. If you don’t have it yet, the process map shows whether it is the right tool for your case and, if it isn’t, we tell you.

  • How long does the process map take?

    Between 2 and 4 weeks, depending on how many areas and people need to be interviewed.

  • What do I get from the map?

    Maps of the current and future process, the measured time of each process, an initial ontology and a prioritised roadmap. It’s yours to keep, even if you don’t continue with us.

  • How do you charge?

    Whichever suits you best: per project, with scope, date and price agreed before we start, or by the hour, if you prefer more flexibility.

  • What happens when a project ends?

    You can carry on with your team, train them with us or book an hours retainer for changes and support, with agreed response times.

  • Do you replace my internal team?

    No. Your team works with us from day one so it can run the system when we are not there.

  • Which systems do you connect to?

    The ones you already have: ERPs such as SAP, databases, spreadsheets, CRM, email and documents.

  • Where do you work?

    From Spain, for organisations in any country. We work remotely and travel when the project needs it.

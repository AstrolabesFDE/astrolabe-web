How we work

Three steps → one goal: making it work in your operation.

  1. Step 1 · 2 to 4 weeks

    Map

    We talk to the people who do the work, draw the ontology of the business and decide what to automate first.

    • We talk to the people doing the work, not just the people managing it.
    • We rank by hours saved, not by how good the demo looks.
  2. Data
    Ontology
    AI

    Step 2 · FDEs inside your operation

    Build

    We build each use case: we fix what’s broken, resolve mismatches between systems and prepare the ontology, with or without Foundry.

    • It often means legacy systems can be retired.
    • We automate with frontier AI such as Claude, or with open-weight models when your AI needs to be sovereign.

  3. Step 3 · ongoing

    Monitor and optimise

    We measure how it performs and whether the team uses it, and keep refining it.

    • Rules get automated; anything that needs judgement stays with a person.
    • We stay with you for as long as it takes.

↻ The cycle repeats with every new use case.

Questions

What people usually ask us.

  • Do I need Palantir Foundry?

    We mostly work in Foundry. If the company doesn’t have it yet, the mapping checks whether it’s the right tool and, if it isn’t, we look for alternatives.

  • How long does the process map take?

    Between 2 and 4 weeks, depending on the number of areas and people involved.

  • How do you charge?

    There are two options: per project, with fixed scope, timeline and price, or by the hour.

  • What happens when a project ends?

    The in-house team can carry on alone, train with us or use a retainer for changes and support, with agreed response times.

  • Do you replace my internal team?

    No. The in-house team works with us from day one, so it can run the system on its own.

  • Which systems do you connect to?

    Whatever is already in place: ERPs such as SAP, databases, spreadsheets, CRM, email and documents.

  • Where do you work?

    We’re based in Spain and work with organisations anywhere in the world. We work remotely and travel when the project needs it.

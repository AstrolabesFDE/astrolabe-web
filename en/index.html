Questions

What people usually ask us.

  • Do I need Palantir Foundry?

    We mostly work on Foundry. If the company doesn’t have it yet, the mapping checks whether it is the right tool and, if not, we look for alternatives.

  • How long does the process map take?

    Between 2 and 4 weeks, depending on the number of areas and people involved.

  • How do you charge?

    By project, with scope, timeline and price fixed up front, or with an hours bank: maintenance hours for what we have built, or free hours for whatever you need.

  • What happens when a project ends?

    Your internal team can carry on alone, train with us, or rely on an hours bank for changes and support, with agreed response times.

  • Do you replace my internal team?

    No. Your internal team works with us from day one so they can run the system on their own.

  • Which systems do you connect to?

    Whatever already exists: ERPs such as SAP, databases, spreadsheets, CRMs, email and documents.

  • Where do you work?

    From Spain, for organisations in any country. We work remotely and travel when the project needs it.

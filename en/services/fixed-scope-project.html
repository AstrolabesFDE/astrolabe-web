Service 02 · Fixed-scope project

From pilot to real operations.

No more glorified demos. We build a use case from start to finish and leave it running in your operation, with scope, timeline and price fixed up front. Measuring its adoption and impact is part of the use case, not an extra.

Who it’s for

  • Companies with a pilot that worked and no idea how to take it to production.
  • Companies that already have Foundry but don’t see the impact: what is used, what isn’t and how much value each use case delivers.
  • Areas with a clear use case: finance, operations, procurement, HR.
  • Companies paying too much for SaaS relative to what they get.

What’s included

  • Integration of the systems involved and resolution of discrepancies between them.
  • The use case’s ontology, ready to grow.
  • An application in Foundry or AIP that your team uses every day.
  • Adoption and impact measurement as a core part of the use case: who uses it, how often and which business metric it moves.
  • An audit of what you already have: we consolidate what is worth keeping and remove what isn’t.
  • SaaS Consolidation: a licence audit and migration with performance measurement to guarantee the service level you had.

How it works

  1. ScopeWe define the use case, the expected result and how we will measure it.
  2. BuildFDE engineers in your operation, working with your team.
  3. Go-liveWe leave it running in production, not in a demo.
  4. Adoption and impactWe measure that it is really used and moves your metrics, and adjust until it does.

What you get

A use case in production, with its adoption and impact measured, and documented. Afterwards your team can carry on alone, train with us or rely on an hours bank.
